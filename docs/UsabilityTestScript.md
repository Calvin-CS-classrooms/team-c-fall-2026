# Usability Test Script

## Introduction

Hello, welcome! I'm [name]. I'm a [school year] studying computer science. Thank
you for agreeing to participate in this usability test for the Calvin Ratings
app. This app provides a way for students to find the best place to study or
socialize, by combining current community reports, place and event information,
and practical context such as capacity, activity, and available resources.

You're here to test our product, so that we can take feedback and improve it as
best we can. We're not testing you, we're testing the app. There's no "right" or
"wrong" answer to anything. We just want to know your experience using this app
and take it into consideration. We'll record your comments and answers, but
won't make them public — it'll only be used for internal analysis and improvement
of our app.

So, we're just going to ask you to do certain tasks, and please think out loud
while you're doing them. If you have any questions, ask, and if you have any
comments, say them. It's all valuable feedback, whether you think something is
cool, frustrating, even stupid or confusing. If you get stuck, tell me and we'll
move on — that's useful feedback.

## Background Questions

- I assume you're a CS108 student? What are you majoring in? Are you minoring in
  anything?
- Where do you usually hang out on campus, to study or to have fun with friends?
- What do you think of the public spaces on campus (variety, busyness, amount of
  spaces, finding those spaces)?
- Have you ever thought it would be nice if you could find spaces by looking them
  up so you know where to go, or would you rather explore and find out yourself?

## The Test

### Reaction to homepage

Go ahead and log in and take a look at the home page. What do you think this
screen is for? What would you tap first, and why?

### General tasks

1. Find a quiet place to study. (US-01, US-14)
2. Find information about Johnny's Cave. (US-02)
3. What's the most recent rating on the explore screen? (US-03)
4. Submit a rating for Peet's Coffee. (US-04)
5. Delete your rating for Peet's Coffee. (US-13)
6. Favorite a spot and find it on your profile. (US-07)
7. You don't see a spot for the lobby in the basement of North Hall. Can you
   suggest that spot to be added? (US-10)

## Summary

- On a scale of 1–5, how easy was that?
- What was the most confusing part?
- Would you use this app?

## Thanks

Thank you so much for being willing to test our app. We hope you found it
interesting and stress-free. We'll contact you soon with a summary of what we
found from all our testers and how we hope to improve it based on your feedback.
