## Vision Statement

**Calvin Ratings** is a campus discovery and decision-making app for Calvin University students and staff. It helps people decide where to go and what to do by combining current community reports, place and event information, and practical context such as capacity, activity, and available resources.

### The Problem

Calvin's official website can provide event details, but it does not consistently show what a place feels like right now, how busy it is, or what a student can use it for. A new student may also know a building's name without knowing its purpose. Generic review and map apps are not designed for Calvin's campus, do not provide Calvin-specific context, and may include people who are not part of the campus community.

### The Product

Calvin Ratings gives Calvin-affiliated users one place to:

- Discover campus locations and events through search, map, and explore views.
- Read concise information about what a location is for, what it offers, and where it is.
- See recent community reports about capacity, activity, and other location-specific conditions.
- Rate a place or event using the rating type that makes sense for it, then browse rating history and trends.
- Save favorite spots, review personal rating history, and compare options before traveling across campus.
- Share anonymous ratings with the campus community while signing in with a Calvin account to verify membership.

### Reliability and Data Freshness

Each Calvin account may submit one active rating per place or event during the defined rating period. A user can update that rating when their experience changes, while the app keeps the newest report and its timestamp visible. Ratings are labeled as recent or outdated and are excluded from the live summary after the freshness window expires. This keeps "live" information meaningful instead of treating old reports as current conditions.

Capacity and seat information will begin as user-reported estimates, supported by timestamps and multiple reports rather than presented as an exact sensor measurement. Future versions could incorporate staff reports, check-ins, or approved occupancy data where those sources are available. The app will communicate this limitation clearly.

### Primary User Stories

- As a student looking for a study space, I want to check a library's recent capacity and activity before walking there so I can choose a useful destination.
- As a new student, I want to search or browse a location and read what it is used for so I can navigate campus confidently.
- As a student planning my day, I want to discover current campus events and see relevant community conditions so I can decide whether to attend.
- As a Calvin-affiliated user, I want to submit one current report for a place or event and update it later so the community sees reliable information without duplicate ratings.
- As a user who has contributed information, I want to see my favorites and rating history so I can return to places I value and understand my activity.

### Differentiation and Success

Calvin Ratings complements, rather than replaces, Calvin's official website: the website remains the source for official event and institutional information, while Calvin Ratings adds timely, student-centered context. It is more useful for campus decisions than a generic review or map service because it is restricted to the Calvin community, uses campus-specific rating categories, explains local places, and makes the age of every live report visible.

The product will succeed when students can answer "Where should I go right now, and what should I expect there?" quickly and confidently, especially when choosing study spaces, social areas, services, and events.
