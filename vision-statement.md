## Vision Statement/ Pitch
- The Calvin Ratings App/ Calvin rates me/  Calvin Ratings
 
This is a platform for Calvin students and staff where they could get to rate and learn about different places/ events at Calvin.
 Unlike the calvin.edu website that may or may not tell you about current campus events, this app gives live ratings to campus events/ places and could also let the users know what the purpose of certain places are on campus.

## Pitch
- It could also be helpful to new students when they need a brief on what certain places are on campus through the place info section.  
- Some requirements of this app are the current location of the user, anonymous live ratings from users and proof of membership to the Calvin institution (to restrict it to only Calvin related users).
- Instance: If you want to go read for finals at the library but you don’t want to go there and realize that all the reading rooms are occupied. 
You could then check the app for the live rating of the **current capacity/ current business** of the library spaces. Assuming we have large amounts of users, we would have users who would have already rated the **capacity** rating on the app. Pending on their rating you could decide whether to go or not to. 
**Note**: the type of rating differs from place to place.
